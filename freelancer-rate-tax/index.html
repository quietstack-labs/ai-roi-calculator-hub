How the math works

Fixed price = hourly rate × hours × (1 + buffer). Platform fee = price × fee %. FX fee applies to what is left after the platform fee. Taxable income = price − both fees; take-home = taxable × (1 − tax %). Withholding (such as Korea's 3.3% on freelance payments) is a prepayment of tax deducted from the gross amount; the calculator shows the cash you receive and whether you owe more or get a refund at filing. Reverse quote = target ÷ ((1 − platform)(1 − FX)(1 − tax)).

FAQ

How do I convert an hourly rate to a fixed price?

Estimate hours honestly, multiply by your rate, then add a buffer for revisions and unknowns (15–30% is common). Fixed pricing rewards you when you work faster than estimated.

What is the 3.3% withholding in Korea?

Payments to individual freelancers in Korea usually have 3.3% (3% income tax + 0.3% local tax) withheld by the payer. It is credited against your annual income tax, so you may owe more or get a refund in May.

What effective tax rate should I enter?

Use your expected total tax divided by your total income for the year, not your top bracket. If unsure, ask a local tax adviser; this tool gives estimates only.
