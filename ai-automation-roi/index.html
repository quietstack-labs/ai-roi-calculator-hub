How the math works

Manual hours = tasks × minutes ÷ 60. Hours saved = manual hours × share automated. Running cost = platform plan + automated tasks × AI cost per task. Net savings = hours saved × staff cost − running cost. Payback = setup ÷ net monthly savings. First-year ROI = (12 × net − setup) ÷ (12 × running cost + setup).

FAQ

What share of a task can AI automation realistically handle?

For structured, rule-based work (copying fields between apps, routing emails, tagging tickets) 70–95% is common. For tasks needing judgment, start at 30–50% and keep a human review step. Use a conservative number; the calculator shows whether the project still pays.

Make vs n8n vs Zapier: which is cheapest?

Make and Zapier charge per operation or task, so cost rises with volume; n8n can be self-hosted for the price of a small server, but you maintain it. Enter the plan you would actually buy in the platform field and compare.

How much does an AI API call cost per task?

A short classification or extraction with a small model is often a fraction of a cent; long documents with a large model can be several cents. Check your provider's pricing for typical input and output length.

What should "loaded staff cost" include?

Wage plus payroll taxes, benefits and overhead. A common rule of thumb is 1.25–1.4 × the hourly wage.
