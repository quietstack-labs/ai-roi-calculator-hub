How the math works

Human cost = minutes × rate per minute + session fees + editing hours × editor rate. AI plan cost = monthly price + minutes above the included amount × overage rate (a plan with no overage is skipped once you exceed it). AI total adds the share of editing you still do. Savings = human cost − cheapest AI total.

FAQ

How much does a human voiceover cost per minute?

Rates vary widely: marketplace freelancers often charge $5–$30 per finished minute, union or studio talent much more, and broadcast use adds usage fees. Use your real quotes.

How are AI voice minutes counted?

Most AI voice tools bill in characters or credits. A finished minute of narration is roughly 900–1,100 characters of English text, so convert your plan's character quota to minutes for this calculator.

Can I use AI voices commercially?

Usually only on paid plans, and voice cloning of a real person needs their consent. Check the provider's terms for your use case.

Will AI replace editing entirely?

No. You still review pronunciation, pacing and mix. That is why the calculator keeps a share of editing cost.
